# Visual directions — moving away from the "AI app" look (Oct 2026)

Why: a similar app (2xGether, launching Nov in DE/AT, incl. Stuttgart) uses the same Lovable/shadcn look:
pink→purple gradients, Inter, icons in tinted rounded squares, soft glass cards, AI stock people.

## What makes I am (IN) look AI-made today
- Violet #8B5CF6 on dark navy with glows and gradients (the Lovable/shadcn default)
- Emoji and lucide icons in headers and lists
- Tiny uppercase letter-spaced labels everywhere
- Same rounded bordered card for everything
- AI-generated people

Rules for any direction: one accent colour, no gradients/glows, no emoji or icon tiles as decoration,
event flyers and real photos carry the colour, black or one-accent pill buttons.
